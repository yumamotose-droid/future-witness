# DEMO_SCRIPT_EN — 90-second demo video, storyboard and narration

Target: Google AI Challenge (90 seconds max, unlisted on YouTube)
Screen: `/deliberate?demo=1` — completes without an API key
Capture: 1440×900, light appearance. No music, or ambient at very low level.

---

## 00:00 – 00:09 ｜ The premise

**Screen** Landing page. Ivory, mostly empty. A hairline draws downward on the
left; "2026" appears in gold, "2050" faint below it. Large mincho type settles
in, one line after the other.

```
未来には、まだ声がない。
The future has no vote yet.
```

**Narration**
> Environment. Urban planning. Energy.
> The people who will live with these decisions longest cannot vote on them today.

**Direction** Do not cut the pause. Nothing hurries here.

---

## 00:09 – 00:18 ｜ Restating the question

**Screen** The deliberation view. The question in mincho:
"Should we clear forest land to build a large commercial complex for economic growth?"
Scroll to "what this question assumes." Stamps press into the margin.

**Narration**
> FUTURE WITNESS starts by doubting the question —
> pulling out what it treats as already settled.

---

## 00:18 – 00:27 ｜ Who can vote, and who cannot

**Screen** The stakeholder list. "Has a vote" in olive, "No vote" in gold.
**Hold on the gold rows**: people living here in 2050, generations not yet born,
species dependent on that slope.

**Narration**
> Who gets to take part in today's decision, and who does not.
> The difference between those two colours is the subject of this project.

**Direction** This is the thesis. Hold three seconds, still.

---

## 00:27 – 00:38 ｜ Evidence, and its limits

**Screen** The EVIDENCE section. "In FY2024 the LULUCF sector was a net sink of
49,421 kt-CO2eq" with the 証 stamp and footnote [1]. **The very next line**:
"the decline is attributed mainly to ageing forests, not to logging volume."

**Narration**
> Every figure arrives with the limitation that belongs to it.
> A correct number quoted without its limits still misleads.

---

## 00:38 – 00:50 ｜ Core samples of the future

**Screen** Three columns of strata fade in; the 2030 / 2040 / 2050 rows line up
across all of them. Scroll slowly to the indicator cross-section (↑ ↓ ⇄).

**Narration**
> One column per option, stacked by decade.
> Read across, and you see exactly where the same indicator diverges.

---

## 00:50 – 01:02 ｜ Testimony from 2050

**Screen** The MIRAI section, stamped 仮 — hypothetical. Text writes itself at
reading speed, caret blinking.

**Narration**
> One person speaks from inside that future.
> It is not a prediction. It is conditional, and the system says so on the label.

**Direction** Stop the narration for 0.8s on the opening sentence so it can be read.

---

## 01:02 – 01:14 ｜ Objecting to ourselves

**Screen** The COUNTER section. Highlight the sentence on screen:

> "The three 2050 narratives render what was lost in sensory detail and what was
> gained in abstractions. That asymmetry works as unearned persuasion toward
> conservation. The reader should distrust this distribution."

**Narration**
> The adversary attacks this system's own staging.
> A machine that will not question how it presents things has not earned trust.

**Direction** The most memorable beat. Large type, two seconds still.

---

## 01:14 – 01:24 ｜ The verification record

**Screen** Close on the numbers.

```
Statements checked               36
Citations resolved               28
Citations removed as non-existent 0
Scenario divergence             100%
```

**Narration**
> Every citation the model offers is checked against the corpus, mechanically.
> One that does not exist is removed before it reaches the screen, and the
> statement is demoted from evidence to inference.
> **The count of what was removed is shown too.**

---

## 01:24 – 01:30 ｜ The decision returns to you

**Screen** "What moved": A → C and the change in certainty. Focus the line
beneath it: "That something moved does not mean the decision improved."
Cut to black.

```
FUTURE WITNESS
The Future Has a Voice.
```

**Narration**
> The system reaches no conclusion.
> The decision is yours, and so is the record.
