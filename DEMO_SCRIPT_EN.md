# DEMO_SCRIPT_EN — 90-second demo video, storyboard and narration

Target: Google AI Challenge (90 seconds max, unlisted on YouTube)
Screen: `/deliberate?demo=1` — no API key needed
Capture: 1440×900, light appearance. No music, or ambient at very low level.

> **Read before shooting.**
> The deliberation itself finishes in **about 11 seconds**, but a **gate** sits in
> the middle of it: nothing below appears until you record where you stand. That
> is not a snag, it is one of the things that makes this different from a chat,
> so it goes **on camera** (00:26–00:34). After the gate, the 2050 testimony
> writes itself at roughly 55 characters a second, then the objection and the
> verification record follow. No speed ramping — the whole thing shoots in real time.

---

## 00:00 – 00:09 ｜ The premise

**Screen** Landing page. Ivory, mostly empty. A hairline draws downward on the
left; "2026" in gold, "2050" faint below it. Large mincho type settles in, one
line after the other.

```
未来には、まだ声がない。
The future has no vote yet.
```

**Narration**
> Environment. Urban planning. Energy. The people who will live with these decisions
> longest cannot vote on them today.

**Direction** Do not cut the pause. The load animation settles at about 2.5s.

---

## 00:09 – 00:17 ｜ Restating the question

**Screen** `/deliberate?demo=1`. The question in mincho. The badge top right
reads **PRE-WRITTEN**, and below it a notice: GEMINI_API_KEY is not set, so this
run uses the scripted engine. Scroll to "what this question assumes." Stamps
press into the margin.

**Narration**
> It starts by doubting the question itself — pulling out what the question treats
> as already settled.

**Direction** Do not hide the PRE-WRITTEN badge. It is evidence of honesty.

---

## 00:17 – 00:26 ｜ Who can vote, and who cannot

**Screen** "Who is affected, and who can vote." HAS A VOTE in olive, NO VOTE in
gold. **Hold on the three gold rows**: people living here in 2050, generations
not yet born, species dependent on that slope.

**Narration**
> Who gets to take part in today's decision, and who does not. The difference
> between those two colours is the subject of this project.

**Direction** The thesis. Three seconds still on the gold rows.

---

## 00:26 – 00:34 ｜ Recording where you stand, first

**Screen** "First, where you stand now." Options A, B and C, a certainty slider,
a field for the reason. **Select A with the cursor and press "Record your
position."** Everything below appears at once.

**Narration**
> It stops here. Before you see any AI output, it records where you stand — so the
> two can be compared afterwards.

**Direction** One slow click. Keep "Read without recording" in frame, so it is
visible that nothing is being forced.

---

## 00:34 – 00:43 ｜ Evidence, and its limits

**Screen** The EVIDENCE section. The first line carries the 証 stamp and
footnote **[2]** — the FY2024 LULUCF net sink of 49,421 kt-CO2eq, 35.5% below
FY1990. **The very next line**, with the same stamp: the decline is attributed
mainly to slower growth in ageing forests and is not a measure of logging volume.

**Narration**
> Every figure arrives with the limitation that belongs to it. A correct number
> quoted without its limits still misleads. The footnotes lead to the full source.

**Direction** The footnote is **[2], not [1]** — [1] is spent earlier, in the
premises. Confirm the number on screen before burning it into a caption.

---

## 00:43 – 00:52 ｜ Core samples of the future

**Screen** "Core samples of the future", with "all hypothetical" in gold at the
right. Three columns of strata, **with the 2030 / 2040 / 2050 rows lined up
across all of them.** Scroll slowly to the indicator table (↑ ↓ ⇄).

**Narration**
> One column per option, stacked by decade. Read across, and you see where the
> same indicator diverges.

**Direction** Frame all three columns at once so the alignment reads. In the
table, the "Reversibility" row (↓ ↑ ⇄) is the clearest.

---

## 00:52 – 01:03 ｜ Testimony from 2050

**Screen** The MIRAI section, 仮 stamped on each. **The text writes itself at
about 55 characters a second, caret blinking** — roughly 3.7 seconds per
passage. It begins the moment the gate opens, so this shoots live.

**Narration**
> One person speaks from inside that future. Not a prediction — a conditional
> hypothetical, and the label says so.

**Direction** Stop the narration for 1.5 seconds while the opening sentence of
the first testimony is written, and let the viewer read it.

---

## 01:03 – 01:14 ｜ Objecting to ourselves

**Screen** The COUNTER section. **Select this sentence on screen (verbatim):**

> An objection to this system itself. All three 2050 narratives render what was
> lost in sensory detail — a smell, fog, birdsong — and what was gained in
> abstractions: jobs, tax revenue, convenience. That asymmetry works as unearned
> persuasion toward conservation. The reader should distrust this distribution.

**Narration**
> The adversary attacks this system's own staging. A machine that will not question
> how it presents things has not earned trust.

**Direction** The most memorable beat. Large type, **two seconds still**. Any
caption must match the screen word for word.

---

## 01:14 – 01:26 ｜ The verification record

**Screen** Close on the numbers.

```
Statements checked                36     Citations resolved      28
Citations removed as non-existent  0     Statements downgraded    0
Scenario divergence             100%     Sources used             7
Generation  scripted:forest-v1 (hand-written, no model call)
```

**Narration**
> Every citation the model offers is checked against the corpus. One that does not
> exist is removed before it reaches the screen, and the statement drops from
> evidence to inference. The count of removals is shown too.

**Direction** Hold one second on "Citations removed as non-existent: 0". The
claim is not that it is zero — it is that the field exists.

---

## 01:26 – 01:30 ｜ The decision returns to you

**Screen** "What moved": A → C and the change in certainty. Focus the line
beneath it: "That something moved does not mean the decision improved."
Cut to black.

```
FUTURE WITNESS
The Future Has a Voice.
```

**Narration**
> The decision is yours, and so is the record.

---

## Shooting notes

- **Do not hide the PRE-WRITTEN badge or the no-key notice.** Both may be in frame.
- **No speed ramping.** The run takes about 11 seconds; the whole video shoots in real time.
- One click at the gate. No stray cursor movement.
- Constant scroll rate. Favour legibility over wide shots.
- Subtitles identical to the narration — add no new claim.
- Anything quoted from the screen must match the screen at the time of shooting.
  Footnote numbers in particular shift with the order of first citation.
