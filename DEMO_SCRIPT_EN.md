# DEMO_SCRIPT_EN — the 90-second demo, in English

> The Japanese script is the one the video is cut to, and it is generated from
> `scripts/video-segments.ts` so it cannot drift from the film
> (`npm run docs:demo`). This file carries the English for the narration and
> the subtitles, in the same order, for an English-language cut or for
> captions on the Japanese one.
>
> The finished video is `artifacts/video/future-witness-demo.mp4`.

Target: Google AI Challenge (90 seconds max, unlisted on YouTube)
Screen: `/deliberate?demo=1`
Structure: 10 segments, 90 seconds

**On shooting.** The deliberation finishes in about eleven seconds in scripted
mode, but a gate sits in the middle of it: nothing below appears until you
record where you stand. That is one of the things that separates this from a
chat, so it goes on camera. The 2050 testimony writes itself at reading speed
the moment the gate opens, which is why the order follows what the screen
actually does. **Nothing is sped up.**

---

## 00:00 – 00:09 ｜ The premise

**Screen** Landing page. A hairline draws down the left; 2026 in gold, 2050
faint below. Large mincho type settles in, line after line.

**Narration**
> Environment. Urban planning. Energy. The people who will live with these
> decisions longest cannot vote on them today.

**Direction** Do not cut the pause.

---

## 00:09 – 00:17 ｜ Restating the question

**Screen** `/deliberate?demo=1`. The badge top right, and the notice under it.
Scroll to "what this question assumes." Stamps press into the margin.

**Narration**
> It starts by doubting the question itself — pulling out what the question
> treats as already settled.

**Direction** Do not hide the mode badge.

---

## 00:17 – 00:26 ｜ Who can vote, and who cannot

**Screen** "Who is affected, and who can vote." Olive for a vote, gold for
none. Hold on the three gold rows.

**Narration**
> Who gets to take part in today's decision, and who does not. The difference
> between those two colours is the subject of this project.

**Direction** The thesis. Three seconds still.

---

## 00:26 – 00:34 ｜ Recording where you stand, first

**Screen** "First, where you stand now." Select an option, press
"Record your position." Everything below appears.

**Narration**
> It stops here. Before you see any AI output, it records where you stand — so
> the two can be compared afterwards.

**Direction** One slow click. Keep "Read without recording" in frame.

---

## 00:34 – 00:45 ｜ Testimony from 2050

**Screen** The MIRAI section, stamped hypothetical. Three testimonies write
themselves at about 55 characters a second, carets blinking.

**Narration**
> One person speaks from inside that future. Not a prediction — a conditional
> hypothetical, and the label says so.

**Direction** Catch it being written. Never speed-ramp this one.

---

## 00:45 – 00:54 ｜ Evidence, and its limits

**Screen** The EVIDENCE section. A figure, and immediately beneath it the
limitation that belongs to it, under the same stamp.

**Narration**
> Every figure arrives with the limitation that belongs to it. A correct
> number quoted without its limits still misleads.

---

## 00:54 – 01:03 ｜ Core samples of the future

**Screen** Three columns of strata, the 2030 / 2040 / 2050 rows lined up
across all of them. Scroll to the indicator table.

**Narration**
> One column per option, stacked by decade. Read across, and you see where
> they diverge.

**Direction** Frame all three columns so the alignment reads.

---

## 01:03 – 01:14 ｜ Objecting to ourselves

**Screen** The COUNTER section. Highlight the self-objection: the narratives
render what was lost in sensory detail and what was gained in abstractions.

**Narration**
> The adversary attacks this system's own staging. A machine that will not
> question how it presents things has not earned trust.

**Direction** The most memorable beat. Two seconds still.

---

## 01:14 – 01:25 ｜ The verification record

**Screen** Close on the numbers: statements checked, citations resolved,
citations removed as non-existent, scenario divergence, the model.

**Narration**
> Every citation the model offers is checked against the corpus. One that does
> not exist is removed before it reaches the screen, and the statement drops
> from evidence to inference. The count of removals is shown too.

**Direction** Hold on the removal count. The claim is that the field exists.

---

## 01:25 – 01:30 ｜ The decision returns to you

**Screen** The position form, then a fade to the end card.

**Narration**
> The decision is yours, and so is the record.

---

## Shooting notes

- Do not hide the mode badge or the notice beneath it.
- No speed ramping; the whole video shoots in real time.
- Subtitles identical to the narration — add no new claim.
- Anything quoted from the screen must match the screen at the time of shooting.
